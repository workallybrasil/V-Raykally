## Built For Local V-Ray Workflows

V-Raykally is a desktop queue manager for artists, freelancers, studios, and
technical directors who render exported `.vrscene` / `.vrs` files with V-Ray
Standalone.

It is intentionally focused: no cloud render dependency, no heavyweight farm
administration, and no source-code distribution. You keep your scenes, logs,
paths, outputs, and render decisions on your own machine.

## Workflow

1. Export one or more scenes from your DCC to `.vrscene` or `.vrs`.
2. Install V-Raykally and point it to your local V-Ray Standalone executable.
3. Add scenes to the queue, reorder priorities, and press **Play**.
4. Track live progress, inspect logs, and retry or adjust jobs when needed.

V-Raykally is a local queue manager. It does not include V-Ray, Chaos assets, or
a Chaos license. You need a valid Chaos/V-Ray installation to render.

V-Raykally is not a cloud render farm or a full worker-fleet scheduler. It is
for local V-Ray queues, batch rendering, overnight renders, progress tracking,
output review, and retry workflows.

## Privacy

- Scenes, paths, outputs, and render logs stay on your machine.
- V-Raykally does not provide cloud rendering or upload your render jobs.
- Local AI diagnostics are designed to run on-device.
- The app works offline except for normal install/download/update flows.

Read the full [Privacy Policy](https://www.v-raykally.online/privacy).
